AlSaahib الصَّاحِب

Free test for adults · iPhone and iPad · no Android yet

Memorize the Quran, a little each day.

Ask for an invite How it works

The test is by invitation. Email support@abwabstudio.com with your first name and the email address you want the TestFlight invite sent to (the one you use for your Apple ID is easiest). If a friend sent you, mention their name. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Each day AlSaahib shows you what to memorize and what to revise, on the page of the Quran. Listen, repeat, then hide the lines and recite from memory.

How to join the test

  1. Email us at support@abwabstudio.com with your first name and the email address for your invite.
  2. Install TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before they are released, from the App Store. Then open our invite on your iPhone and tap Install.
  3. Use AlSaahib for a few days, as much as suits you. Then tell us what confused you, what broke and what you liked: take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or just email us.

What you do each day

You set up your plan once: how much time you have each day and what you already know. Then the app does the planning.

Memorize and revise

A small portion, every day

Memorize today gives you a few new verses. Tap Start memorizing and they open on the page, with the rest of the page faded. When you know them, tap I've memorized this.

Revise today brings back a page you already know, so it stays with you. Recite it, then tap Done revising.

The Quran page

Listen, repeat, hide

Turn the pages as in a printed copy of the Quran (a mushaf). Tap Play to hear the recitation of Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil al-Husary (may Allah have mercy on him), verse by verse. Turn on Repeat to keep it going while you learn by ear.

Then turn on Hide. The lines are covered and you recite from memory. Unsure of a line? Tap it to see just that line.

Your notes

Write on the page, in layers

Mark the page as you would a printed copy with a pencil: a pen, a highlighter, or a written note on a word.

Each time you go over a page you can start a new layer, like a clear sheet on top of the page. Hide or show each layer with a tap, and mark a note resolved once it is fixed. The Notes tab lists every page you have marked.

With a teacher

A teacher is optional. You are paired only with a teacher of your own gender, and we approve every teacher ourselves before they can teach.

  • Calls are 10 minutes, audio only. No video.
  • Your teacher can show you the page or hide it, so you recite from memory.
  • As you recite, your teacher marks slips on the words of the page: one color for tajweed (the rules of recitation), another for a memorization slip. You see the marks as they are made.
  • After the call you can see your teacher's marks and a short note.
  • During the test we pair students and teachers by hand, and places are limited, so we cannot promise everyone a teacher.

How to ask for a teacher

The test

What it is

  • Free. Nothing is charged during the test.
  • For adults, 18 or older.
  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, through Apple's TestFlight app.
  • You make an account with your email address and a password.

Before you start

  • This is an early version. Your plan and notes are saved on your phone only, and a new test build could lose them, so keep a copy of anything you cannot redo.
Known gaps in this version
  • There is no Settings screen yet. To change your setup answers, email us.
  • The Learn tab says "Coming soon", and Visuals on the Quran page is greyed out and not ready yet.
  • Only the standard Madani page for now. The IndoPak page is coming.
  • Community (sending a recitation to another student to check) is not in this test.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google is not available yet.

Your information

  • Your email: used to log you in and to reply to you. Never sold or shared for ads. Emails you send us are deleted after one year.
  • Your voice: in a teacher call, your teacher hears it live. It is never recorded or stored.
  • Your name: a teacher sees a name made from your email address (the part before the "@"), not your email address itself.
  • Your plan and notes are kept on your phone. Your account is kept on our server when the test ends, so it carries over to the released app. You can delete it in the app at any time.
