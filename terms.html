Test terms

Last updated 23 November 2026. These terms apply to the test and may change before release.

Thank you for testing AlSaahib. These terms are an agreement between you and Abwab Studio, the small independent studio that makes the app ("we"). By creating an account or using the test version, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. A free test

  • The test version is free. We do not charge you anything during the test.
  • It is unfinished. Features may change, break or be removed. Your plan, progress or notes could be lost between builds.
  • A TestFlight build stops working when Apple expires it, or when we end the test.
  • When the test ends, your account is kept so it carries over to the released app, unless you delete it.
  • Apple's TestFlight terms also apply to how you get and use test builds.

2. Who may use it

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You need an account: an email address you can receive mail at, and a password. Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • One account per person. Do not make an account for someone else.

3. Teacher calls

  • Calls are 10 minutes, audio only, between one student and one teacher.
  • We add every teacher ourselves, and we pair students with teachers by hand. Students are paired with a teacher of the same gender.
  • Being in the test does not promise you a teacher or a number of calls. Places are limited.
  • Teachers help you practice. We cannot promise how much you will memorize or how quickly.

4. How to behave

Please use AlSaahib with the same respect you would show in a mosque class. In particular:

  • Be respectful and modest in calls. No insults, harassment, or sexual or hateful talk.
  • Do not record a call in any way (screen recording, another phone, or any other tool), and do not share what someone says in a call without their permission. The app does not record calls, and neither may you. Screenshots or screen recordings of the app to report a problem are fine, but not while a call is live.
  • Do not ask a teacher or student for their phone number, social media or other contact outside the app.
  • Do not try to break, overload, copy or get around the security of the app or its server, or reach other people's data.
  • Do not use the app for anything unlawful.

If someone behaves badly in a call, email support@abwabstudio.com with the date and time of the call. We read these first.

  • The translations, the Quran font (Amiri Quran, SIL Open Font License) and other materials belong to their owners and are used under their licenses.
  • If you see a mistake in the Quran text, please tell us straight away.
