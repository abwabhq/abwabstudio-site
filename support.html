Support

Email us at support@abwabstudio.com. A person reads every message, and we aim to reply within 2 working days. Please write from the email address you signed up with, so we can find your account.

How to get help

  • By email: support@abwabstudio.com.
  • Through TestFlight: take a screenshot while using the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open the TestFlight app, choose AlSaahib and tap Send Beta Feedback. If the app crashed, TestFlight may ask you to send a crash report; please say yes and add a line about what you were doing.
  • A problem in a teacher call (someone was rude, or asked for your contact details): email us with the date and time of the call. We read these first.

What to put in a report

  1. What you were trying to do.
  2. What happened instead, and any message the app showed (word for word if you can).
  3. The steps to make it happen again, if you know them.
  4. A screenshot or screen recording of the app. These are fine, but not while a call is live: never record a teacher call.
  5. Your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version (Settings → General → About), and the AlSaahib version (shown at the bottom of your profile in the app).
  6. For a problem in the Quran text: the surah, the verse or page number, and what you see.

Please do not send your password. We will never ask for it.

Deleting your account

In the app: tap the person icon on Home to open your profile, tap Delete account, then Delete my account. Or email support@abwabstudio.com from the address you signed up with. We will delete it within 7 days and email you when it is done.

This deletes your account and what our server keeps for it, including your teacher calls and what was saved from them. It cannot be undone. Your plan, progress and notes on your phone are not deleted; delete the app to remove them. The privacy policy has the full list, including the few things that stay.

Common questions

  • Joining the test
  • Your account
  • Teacher calls
  • Your notes and data

How do I get the app?

The test is by invitation. Email support@abwabstudio.com with your first name and the email address you want the invite sent to (the one you use for your Apple ID is easiest). If a friend sent you, mention their name. We usually reply within 2 working days.

When we invite you, install TestFlight (Apple's free app for trying apps before they are released) from the App Store, then open the invitation on your iPhone or iPad and tap Install.

Which devices work?

iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. There is no Android version yet.

Does it cost anything?

No. The test is free and the app does not ask for payment. We have not set prices for after the test, and nothing you do in the test will charge you.

What happens when the test ends?

Your account is kept, with what our server holds for it (such as your teacher calls), so it carries over to the released app. You can delete your account at any time. What is only on your phone (your plan and notes) stays there as long as you keep the app.

I didn't get the email to confirm my account.

Look in your spam or junk folder, and in "Promotions" or "Other" tabs. The email comes from no-reply@mail.abwabstudio.com. Open it on the same iPhone or iPad you signed up on, and tap the link: it opens the app and logs you in. If it is not there after a few minutes, tap Resend email in the app (it waits a minute between sends), or Use a different email. If it still does not come, email us from the same address and we will confirm your account for you.

I forgot my password.

On the log-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. Open the email on the same iPhone or iPad and tap the link: the app opens and asks for a new password (at least 6 characters). Check your spam folder if it does not arrive. If the link does not work, email us from the address you signed up with.

How do I get a teacher?

During setup, choose Add the teacher program, or later tap the teacher card on Home. The app asks your birth year and gender, and when you would like your calls. We then pair you with a teacher by hand. Places in the test are limited, and we may not be able to give everyone a teacher. When you have a teacher, your next call shows on Home. Calls are 10 minutes and audio only.

How long does it take to get a teacher?

There is no set time. It depends on which teachers are free at the times you chose. You can email us to ask.

Who are the teachers, and how are they checked?

You are paired only with a teacher of your own gender. We add every teacher ourselves: a teacher can join only by our invitation or after we approve them. Teachers agree to the same rules as everyone (no recording calls, no asking for contact details).

Can I choose a male or female teacher?

You are paired with a teacher of your own gender. Choosing a particular teacher is not offered yet.

Why does the app ask my birth year and gender?

Only when you join the teacher program. Your birth year shows us you are 18 or older; the teacher program is for adults. Your gender lets us pair you with a teacher of the same gender, which is our rule. We ask once, and gender cannot be changed later, so please answer carefully. Both are kept on our server until you delete your account.

What if the call drops?

The app tries to reconnect by itself and shows "Reconnecting". If that does not work, tap Join again. If you cannot get back in, email us with the date and time of the call.

Is my recitation recorded?

No. Teacher calls are live audio only and are not recorded. Your teacher hears you while the call is on, and then the sound is gone. What is saved is your teacher's marks on the page, a short note, ratings and your next passage, so you can see them after the call. Please do not record calls yourself either.

What can my teacher see about me?

Your display name (the part of your email address before the "@"), your gender, your time zone, and your calls with them. Not your email address, your birth year, your own plan or your notes. The privacy policy has the details.

Will I lose my notes?

Your plan, progress and notes are kept only on this phone. They are not on a new phone if you log in there, and deleting the app removes them. They are included in your iPhone's own backup if you use one. A new test build could also lose them, so please do not keep anything there you cannot do without.

Does the app work offline?

Your plan, the Quran pages and your notes work without the internet. Logging in, teacher calls and recitation audio you have not played before need the internet.
