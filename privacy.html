AlSaahib privacy policy (test version)

Last updated 23 November 2026.

This page explains what the AlSaahib test version collects, why, where it is kept and for how long. It is written for people testing the app through TestFlight, Apple's app for trying apps before they are released. If something here is unclear, email us.

In short

  • Your email is used to log you in and to reply to you. We never sell it or share it for ads. A teacher sees a name made from it (the part before the "@"), not the address itself.
  • Your voice in a teacher call goes live to your teacher. It is never recorded or stored.
  • Emails you send us are kept for one year, then deleted.
  • When the test ends, testers' accounts and what our server holds for them are kept, so they carry over to the released app. Your plan and notes stay on your phone.
  • To delete your account, tap the person icon on Home, then Delete account. Or email us. No ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.

Who runs the app

AlSaahib is made and run by Abwab Studio ("we"), a small independent studio. We decide how your data is used.

We are reached by email only: support@abwabstudio.com. A person reads every message.

Adults only

The test version is for people aged 18 or over. When you create an account you tick "I am 18 or older". The teacher program also asks your birth year and does not accept anyone under 18. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Who at AlSaahib can see your data

The person who runs Abwab Studio is the administrator and can see all the data on our server, to run the test, pair students with teachers and handle reports. Nobody else at AlSaahib has access.

What your teacher can see

A teacher you are paired with sees:

  • your display name, your gender and your time zone;
  • your calls with them: times, the page, their marks, notes and ratings, and your progress from those calls.

Your teacher does not see your email address, your birth year, your own plan or your notes. Teachers agree not to record calls or share what is said in them (see the test terms).

What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or calendar.
  • No advertising identifier, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics.
  • No payment details: the test is free.
  • No recordings of calls.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

What stays on your phone

These are kept only on your phone, in the app's own storage. They are not sent to our server in this test version:

  • Your own memorization plan and your progress in it (what you have reviewed and memorized).
  • Your notes: the note layers and the marks you add to pages and verses.
  • Your answers from the first setup: your goals, minutes a day, how well you read, how much you know of the rules of recitation, the script you chose, and which parts of the Quran you have memorized and how sure you are of each.
  • That you ticked "I am 18 or older", with the date.
  • Your daily reminder choice. The daily reminder and the call reminders (15 and 2 minutes before a teacher call) are set on your phone, only if you allow notifications.
  • Recitation audio you have played, kept for a while so it plays again without the internet.

Because these are on the phone only, they are not on a new phone if you log in there, and they are not removed when you delete your account. Deleting the app removes them. They may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backup, like other apps' data. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app may also keep a small copy of your plan and of which verses you know in your own iCloud account, so it can be restored if you reinstall. We cannot see your iCloud data.

Microphone and notifications

  • Microphone. Used only during a teacher call, so your teacher can hear you. Your iPhone asks your permission first, and you can turn it off in the Settings app.
  • Notifications. Used for your daily reminder and for call reminders, only if you allow them. They are set on your phone.

Sending a recitation to another student to check is not part of this test version. If a later version adds it, we will update this page first.

If you apply to teach

If you teach in the test, we also keep your gender, the languages you teach in, whether you hold an ijazah (a license to teach recitation), a short bio, your time zone and the hours you can teach, so we can pair you with students. These are kept on our server until you delete your account.

Where your data is stored

Our server is in the United States. If you live outside the United States, your data is sent there. Call audio passes through LiveKit's servers while the call is live.

How long we keep data

  • Account, teacher program and call data: until you delete your account. Your calls with a teacher are also removed if that teacher deletes their account.
  • When the test ends: we keep testers' accounts and what our server holds for them, so your account carries over to the released app and you can carry on where you left off. You can delete your account at any time, during the test or after it.
  • Emails you send us: one year, then we delete them.
  • Backups: if our host keeps backup copies of our database, deleted data can stay in those copies until they are replaced.
  • Sign-in log: Supabase's log of sign-ups, log-ins and password resets is kept for a limited time set by Supabase. It is not removed when you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app: tap the person icon on Home to open your profile, tap Delete account, then Delete my account. Or email support@abwabstudio.com from the address you signed up with. We will delete it within 7 days and email you when it is done.

Deleting your account removes, from our server:

  • your sign-in (email and password) and your display name;
  • your birth year, gender, time zone and call preferences;
  • your calls and everything saved from them: marks, notes, ratings, passages and the progress worked out from them;
  • your notification records and call-quality numbers;
  • your teaching details, if you teach.

What stays:

  • safety reports, no longer linked to your account;
  • an internal log that actions happened (for example "an account was deleted"), under an account number that no longer leads to you. It holds no email, name or content;
  • Supabase's sign-in log and backups, for the limited time above;
  • everything on your phone (your plan, progress and notes). Delete the app to remove those.

Deletion cannot be undone.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what our server holds about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@abwabstudio.com. We will answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also be able to complain to the privacy regulator where you live.

This website

This website (abwabstudio.com) has no cookies, analytics or ads. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and its date before the change reaches you, and tell testers in the TestFlight notes for that build.
