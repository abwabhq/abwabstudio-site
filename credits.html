Recitation

The recitation is by Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil al-Husary (may Allah have mercy on him), Murattal, Hafs. It is streamed verse by verse from Islamic Network under the alquran.cloud terms, and kept for a while on your phone. The recordings remain the copyright of their rights holders.

The word-by-word highlighting uses timings from quran-align by Collin Fair, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. We adjusted a few verses where it counts words differently, and verses it could not time are highlighted as a whole.

Translations of the meanings

Shown exactly as issued, from QuranEnc.com, under its terms.

  • English (Saheeh International): Issued by Noor International Center. Version 1.1.2, updated 24 June 2025. Source
  • Urdu: Translated by Muhammad Ibrahim Junagarhi, developed under the supervision of the Rowwad Translation Center. The original translation is available for the purpose of expressing opinion, evaluation, and continuous development. Version 1.1.3, updated 13 August 2025. Source
  • Indonesian: Issued by the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs. Developed under the supervision of Rowwad Translation Center. The original translation is available for the purpose of expressing opinion, evaluation, and continuous development. Version 1.0.1, updated 23 June 2025. Source
  • French: Translated by Rashid Ma’ash (also written Rachid Maach). Version 1.0.3, updated 21 June 2026. Source
